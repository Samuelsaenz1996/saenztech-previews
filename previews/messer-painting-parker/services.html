Floor finishes

Epoxy that makes garages easy.

Epoxy is an affordable, durable and good-looking option for concrete. It protects the slab and cleans up easily, which helps with slush on snowy days. Polyurea coatings are available for commercial floors.

  • Garages, basements and shops
  • Industrial-grade epoxy
  • Polyurea for commercial spaces
  • Custom colors and decorative finishes

Wood finish

Stain that protects and glows.

Stain adds a protective layer that helps keep moisture out and shields wood from UV. We stain inside and out, from decks and fences to doors and wine racks, with custom stain matching.

  • Decks, fences and pergolas
  • Doors, cabinets and built-ins
  • Custom stain color matching
  • Seasonal deck maintenance

Sandblasting

Back to bare, without the damage.

Also known as abrasive blasting, this method strips materials of all kinds, removing build-up, paint, rust and calcium. A dustless option is available, and it works well for graffiti removal and prep before paint or stain.

  • Paint, rust and calcium removal
  • Dustless blasting available
  • Graffiti removal
  • Surface prep before coating
MetalBrickConcreteWood
Power washing

Clean surfaces, protected waterways.

Power washing is a fast way to remove dust, dirt and grime that builds up around the outside of your home. We recapture contaminated water to help protect local waterways.

  • Siding, decks and driveways
  • Pre-paint cleaning
  • Contaminated water recapture

Questions

Good to know.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Use the instant estimator for a ballpark, then call or text (303) 903-0500 to schedule a free in-person estimate.

What’s included in the price?

All services include high-quality paint, materials and labor. Your written quote lists the exact scope.

Do I need to move out for interior painting?

No. We paint occupied homes too. The estimator lets you choose occupied or unoccupied, since furniture and daily life change the prep involved.

Is the garage door included with an exterior?

Yes. Garage doors and painted front doors are included with a full exterior body paint. Front door staining or refinishing is quoted separately.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Messer Painting is fully licensed and insured.
